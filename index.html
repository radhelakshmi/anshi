The examination will cover the complete syllabus of each subject and follow the CBSE syllabus, prescribed question-paper pattern and competency-based/Higher Order Thinking approach, wherever applicable.

Important Examination Rules

  1. Report before the scheduled examination time.
  2. Carry required stationery and examination materials.
  3. Follow the prescribed CBSE syllabus and paper pattern.
  4. Each paper covers the complete syllabus of the respective subject.
  5. Follow instructions of the examination coordinator/invigilator.
  6. Mobile phones and unauthorised electronic devices are not permitted.
  7. Maintain discipline and silence inside the examination hall.
  8. No unfair means or unauthorised material is permitted.
  9. Manage time carefully and attempt questions according to the prescribed pattern.
  10. Revise the complete syllabus, important concepts, competency-based questions and previous examination trends.
  11. Any required schedule change will be communicated officially by RL-33 management.
  12. All members are expected to maintain RL-33's academic standards.

Final Note

The Annual Examination is based on the complete syllabus and is intended to assess conceptual understanding, application, analytical ability and overall examination readiness.

How to Conquer Board Exams

From: Priya Gupta

To: All RL-33 Members

Date: 22 September 2026

RL-33 Complete Academic Plan — 20 October to 31 March

The plan follows five phases designed to complete the syllabus, revise systematically, practise questions, conduct examinations and prepare for the CBSE Board Examination.

Goal: NO BACKLOG • NO LAST-MINUTE PANIC • COMPLETE REVISION • CONSISTENT PRACTICE

PHASE 1 — 50 DAYS COMPLETE REVISION

20 October – 06 December

Target: Complete revision of the full syllabus across all subjects and identify weak areas.

  • Monday: History + Economics — 4 Chapters
  • Tuesday: History + Economics — 4 Chapters
  • Wednesday: History + Economics — revised chapter question practice + Sociology — 1 Chapter
  • Thursday: Political Science + Geography — 4 Chapters
  • Friday: Political Science + Geography — 4 Chapters
  • Saturday: Political Science + Geography — revised chapter question practice + Sociology Chapter Revision
  • Sunday: Weekly Test + Performance Analysis
  • English: 4 days/week × 1 hour
  • Hindi: 2 days/week × 1 hour

PHASE 2 — PRE-BOARD EXAMINATION

07 December – 31 December

  • RL-33 Pre-Board + School Pre-Board — Parallel Preparation
  • Analyse performance after every paper
  • Identify weak chapters and recurring mistakes
  • Improve answer writing, speed and time management
  • Use Pre-Board performance to plan the next revision

Target: Convert examination mistakes into improvement before the final revision phase.

PHASE 3 — MASTER REVISION + SAMPLE PAPERS

01 January – 28 January

  • Week 1: History + Geography — complete master revision and full-length sample papers.
  • Week 2: Political Science + Economics — complete master revision and full-length sample papers.
  • Week 3: English + Hindi — complete master revision and full-length sample papers.
  • Week 4: Sociology + Physical Education — complete master revision and full-length sample papers.
  • Every Sunday: Weak Area + Difficult Concept Revision.

Target: Every member enters the RL-33 Annual Examination with complete revision and full-paper practice.

PHASE 4 — RL-33 ANNUAL EXAMINATION

29 January – 10 February

Complete syllabus • CBSE-oriented pattern • Full-length examination practice

This phase serves as the final academic checkpoint before the Board Examination.

PHASE 5 — FINAL BOARD EXAMINATION PREPARATION

11 February – 31 March

  • Revision only
  • No new topics
  • No new syllabus
  • Focus on weak areas
  • Revise important concepts repeatedly
  • Practise according to the gap between Board papers
  • Maintain answer-writing speed and accuracy
  • Follow the official CBSE examination schedule

Final Target: Every RL-33 member enters the CBSE Board Examination fully prepared, revised and examination-ready.

RL-33 Board Examination Mantra

COMPLETE → REVISE → PRACTISE → TEST → ANALYSE → IMPROVE → REVISE AGAIN

20 OCTOBER → 31 MARCH
One Plan. Five Phases. Complete Preparation.
